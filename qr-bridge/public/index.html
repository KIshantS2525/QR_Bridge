QR·Bridge

Two screens. One blinking code.

Type a message, record a clip, or pick an image on one device — it leaves as a stream of QR codes and gets rebuilt on the other, entirely through the camera. No file ever touches a network.

💻

This is the laptop

Write text, record audio, or choose an image — turn it into a QR code (or a rapid sequence of them) to display on screen.
📱

This is the phone

Point the camera at the other screen. It decodes what it sees and rebuilds the message, clip, or picture live.
